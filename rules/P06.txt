The U-Boat Actions

The actions available are as follows:

MOVE

Surf | Peri | Med | Deep
1 | 2 | 3 | 3

Roll 3 dice (2 dice if Engine is Damaged); AP this turn = highest die rolled + 1 AP if Captain is alive

MOVE — Can only enter a Ship’s hex if Medium/Deep. Can only enter shallows if Surfaced/Periscope

Each action allows your U-Boat to Move one hex in the direction it is currently Facing. The U-Boat cannot enter a hex which:

➤ Is a Land hex.
➤ Is a Shallow Sea hex unless the U-Boat is Surfaced or at Periscope Depth.
➤ Has a Ship in it unless the U-Boat is at a Depth level of Medium or Deep.

These are all pretty obvious and very memorable, although the last 2 bullets do have a reminder in the Chart.

TURN

Surf | Peri | Med | Deep
1 | 1 | 2 | 3

Roll 3 dice (2 dice if Engine is Damaged); AP this turn = highest die rolled + 1 AP if Captain is alive

TURN — 1 hex edge left or right

Each action allows your U-Boat to Turn one hex edge (60 degrees) to the left or right of where it is currently Facing. Facing is important as it determines where the U-Boat will Move to under that Action above, but also where it will Fire its Torpedoes under that Action below.

CHANGE DEPTH

Surf | Peri | Med | Deep
2 | 1 | 2 | 1

Roll 3 dice (2 dice if Engine is Damaged); AP this turn = highest die rolled + 1 AP if Captain is alive

CHANGE DEPTH — 1 depth level (once per turn) (cannot go below Peri in shallows / above Med beneath ships)

This action allows your U-Boat to Change Depth to an ‘adjacent’ Depth (up or down). Note that to Dive down to the next Deeper level always costs 2 APs, but to Ascend to the level above costs just 1 AP. Also, note that you can only change your Depth by one level at a time and only once per turn (you cannot therefore Dive or Ascend more than once, and you cannot Dive then Ascend or Ascend then Dive on the same turn).

Finally, there is a reminder that in Shallow Sea your U-Boat can only ever be at Periscope Depth or on the Surface, and when it is under a Ship (in the same hex as a Ship) it can only be at Medium or Deep. Your U-Boat simply cannot enter Shallow water unless it is already Surfaced or at Periscope Depth, and it cannot choose to enter an enemy Ship’s hex if it is not at Medium or Deep. If an enemy Ship enters the same hex as the U-Boat when the U-Boat is not at Medium or Deep, then the U-Boat will undertake a ‘Forced Dive’ which will be explained below when considering the movement of enemy Ships.
